# 📊 Sales Analytics & Business Insights Project

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive sales data analysis aimed at uncovering actionable business insights related to product performance, customer behavior, and marketing effectiveness.

The analysis combines **SQL, Python (Pandas), and data visualization tools** to answer real-world business questions and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Identify top-performing and underperforming products
* Analyze year-over-year sales trends
* Detect customer drop-offs in the purchase funnel
* Evaluate effectiveness of promotional campaigns
* Compare category-wise performance over time

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Python** (Pandas, Matplotlib)
* **SQL** (Data extraction & transformation)
* **Jupyter Notebook**
* **Google Looker Studio**
* **HTML / CSS Dashboard**

---

## 🔍 Key Analyses Performed

### 1. Product Performance Analysis

* Identified top-selling products in high-demand categories
* Ranked products based on total sales volume

### 2. Sales Decline Investigation

* Compared sales across periods (2021 vs 2022)
* Highlighted products with significant performance drop

### 3. Customer Behavior Analysis

* Identified users who completed checkout but did not convert
* Provided insights for retargeting strategies

### 4. Campaign Effectiveness Analysis

* Compared weekend vs weekday sales performance
* Evaluated impact of promotional campaigns

### 5. Category Trend Analysis

* Performed time-series analysis across categories
* Identified high-growth and seasonal trends

 💡 Key Insights

* Weekend campaigns show measurable impact on sales performance
* Certain categories demonstrate strong seasonal and upward trends
